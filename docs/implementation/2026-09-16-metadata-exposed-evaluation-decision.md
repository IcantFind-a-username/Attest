# Decision: a separate metadata-exposed evaluation stratum

Status: proposed; no selection, hidden-truth access or execution authorized by this file.
Existing local/temporary-directory/paid permissions remain valid. This asks only
about a new sampling rule after D-275's qualification shortfall.

## Why another decision exists

The D-274 study is closed without replacement. Its minimum remains three natural
regressions and three controls across two repositories; no qualified population
exists. Its protocol expressly prohibits automatic expansion after failure.
AGENTS §16 reserves outcome-dependent sample/exclusion/retry changes to the owner.
The current repository audit shows that installation screening records exclude
otherwise potentially unused cases. Screening is exposure, but is not itself a
product evaluation. Loading the original dataset may also have loaded hidden
columns; absence of a recorded probe cannot prove universal lack of exposure.

## One decision

**Allow a separate, explicitly metadata-exposed study?** Recommended: yes, with
these fixed limits. If not approved, preserve the qualification blocker and do
not spend on an ineligible population. This is not a request to reauthorize paid
calls, temporary directories or the previous study.

- Keep the same pinned SWE-bench Verified revision, existing held-out split,
  contemporary date cutoff, product version, original-test witness requirement,
  semantic reviews, minimum, hard spend cap, and report-then-stop boundary.
- Audit existing records before source or hidden-truth access. Permit a prior
  reference only when its fields and producing code establish installation
  metadata screening alone. Exclude all known product runs, probes, semantic
  reviews, development cases, all previously selected cases, and uncertain
  provenance. Preserve every exclusion and the audit's scope limitation.
- Before new truth access, commit a selection driver and protocol. Order eligible
  repositories by SHA-256 of `attest-metadata-exposed-v1|<repo>`; take the first
  four with at least three eligible cases. Within each, order by SHA-256 of
  `attest-metadata-exposed-v1|<repo>|<instance_id>` and take up to five. Maximum
  twenty. Retain shortfalls; no backfill or second batch after outcomes.
- Freeze natural controls by the existing bounded ancestor/hash procedure before
  reading their diffs. Keep original-test parent PASS / introducing-head FAIL,
  repeated execution, no repair reversal, and product-blind semantic review.
  Do not weaken the product, isolation or extraction boundary to qualify cases.
- Report as **metadata-exposed, previously unevaluated cases within the audited
  records**, not unseen repositories or an untouched holdout. No promotion to a
  release gate or claim of universal zero false positives follows.
- Paid dispatch still requires the exact frozen units, qualified minimum, same-
  workflow free artifact smoke, durable precharge/checkpoints and reconciled
  headroom. No cap increase or automatic population expansion is included.

The source pool may again fail qualification. This approval would authorize one
bounded attempt, not guarantee a paid report. Evidence for the preceding result:
[qualification report](../acceptance/2026-09-16-case-heldout-qualification.md).
