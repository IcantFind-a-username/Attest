# Final remainder study: terminal qualification shortfall

## Result

The owner-authorized final remainder study cannot dispatch paid evaluation. Both
independent source reviewers agreed on six natural forward regression candidates,
but every parent/head tree failed the frozen tracked-symlink export rule before
extraction. No dependency installation, original test, product review or model API
call followed. **Product recall and precision remain unmeasured by this study.**
This is a corpus compatibility failure, not zero recall or evidence of correct silence.

The 12 export refusals are revision attempts for six cases, not 12 defects. The six
pairs are Django 17084, 15380, 15554 and Matplotlib 22719, 26291, 24177; exact SHAs
and every source refusal are retained in the linked qualification record. The two
reviewers agree on all six pairs. No exact pair repeats among them. Runtime failure
precludes admission regardless of any further semantic-duplication analysis.
Unresolved history includes bounded partial-clone history timeouts and does not
prove the absence of an existing contract or natural regression.

## Boundary and stopping decision

The unchanged minimum is three defects and three controls across at least two
repositories. None of the six source pairs can reach that gate under this protocol.
Control execution was not started once this prerequisite became impossible. The
study retains all forty control judgments and does not replace or resample anything.
No paid smoke or dispatch was attempted; no apparent zero can be scored as a
true negative, precision estimate or universal false-positive guarantee.

Representative refused links include Django's docs/_theme/djangodocs-epub/static
image links and Matplotlib's mpl-data/images symbolic SVG links. The exporter
refuses all tracked links without judging their destination. This observation does
not prove those links unsafe or prove that deleting them would preserve behavior.
The protocol expressly froze this conservative rule before source qualification.

This authorized final batch ends here, as its stopping rule requires. A future
measurement needs a separately authorized protocol for safely exporting in-tree
relative symlinks (with escape/cycle/dangling-link checks and unchanged source
semantics), then fresh qualification evidence. That is the minimal next decision;
another sample expansion, more model spending or a lower sample floor does not
repair the demonstrated prerequisite. No such change or retry is included here.
