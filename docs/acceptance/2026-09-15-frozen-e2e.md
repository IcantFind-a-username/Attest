## 1. What was frozen, and what could not be

The paid runs uploaded their **ledgers only**; neither the candidate store nor the model's responses
left the runner, so no recorded proposal exists verbatim -- no claim text, no anchor line, no
non-eligible candidate. `FrozenProvider` answers a review from what the ledger keeps:

- **proposals**: for each change unit, every recorded *eligible* candidate (unit, symbol, cluster size
  from `verification_ranking.order`), repeated across the K = 5 samples up to its cluster size,
  anchored by a fixed rule (the first hunk line inside the recorded symbol on head, an added line
  first) and named by one token unique to it, so the product's own dedup rebuilds the clusters;
- **model probes**: the probe the recorded search ended on (`probe_observation`), served once --
  earlier attempts of a search, and every attempt of a search that found no differential, were never
  recorded, so those calls are refused and the search ends;
- **cost**: each call charged the recorded per-call tokens of its stage. Each review's proposer
  attempt cache is moved aside first (run `f1` found that the second arm otherwise replayed the
  first's identical prompts for free and had a larger budget; §5).

Fidelity, checked per review against the recorded ranking:

| population | eligible candidates rebuilt | differences |
|---|---|---|
| forty | 40 of 40 cases identical in unit and count | `more-itertools-guard_raise-05`: recorded cluster of 7, rebuilt 5 (two members per sample cannot be rebuilt); `more-itertools-guard_raise-04`: the recorded ranking row had no symbol, the rebuilt one names `first`. One candidate each; no effect on order |
| controls | 43 of 44 | `loguru#1510`: the anchor rule landed in a *new* `__call__` among several, and the candidate became new code; `boltons#467`: a cluster of 6 rebuilt as 5 |

**What cannot be replayed, and so is not measured**: the original proposals themselves (claim text,
anchor line, every non-eligible candidate); the text-level dedup of those claims (reconstructed
claims cluster exactly as recorded, by construction); and every failed probe attempt of a recorded
search, including what the model would propose as a second or third probe.

## 6. What is and is not claimed, and what the next decisions rest on

- **The three certifications D-253 found by hand arise from the product path on its own**, with no
  model probe, on identical frozen input, and nothing the shipped arm published is lost.
- **Budget, ranking, reconstructed dedup and the publication cap did not change whether any case of
  this run was hit**, in either arm; on real traffic the budget and the cap cut the same candidates
  in both arms. That is a statement about these cases, not a finding that the caps cost nothing.
- **The rule reaches few cases.** It built a probe in 8 of the forty and in 2 of the 24 control pull
  requests: unittest-style assertions (`assertEqual`, `assertRaises`), a `with raises` around the
  touched symbol itself rather than a caller, fixtures and classes defined inside a test are all
  outside it -- the decision on extending contract kinds rests on this list.
- **The search gap needs the model's search observed again.** 7 of the forty and 11 control
  candidates end because the recorded search never kept its failed attempts; a real model gets up to
  three. What the experimental search does there can only be seen by running the model search anew.
- **Zero red publications on 24 independent control pull requests** is a count with an upper bound of
  13.8%, not a rate; v6 admitted no contract on any of them. The 7 value notes on 4 of them are the
  yellow observation line, written identically in both arms, and are not part of that count.
