## 3. Condition 4, stated rather than softened

**The number is 2 of 28 — 7.1%, Wilson 95% [2.0%, 22.6%]** on a held-out corpus rebuilt so every
case declares an interpreter the product supports ([report](2026-09-12-heldout-supported.md)).
`G-RECALL-002` asks for **≥70% point detection with a ≥50% lower bound**. **Both clauses fail,
and the interval's upper bound is 22.6%** — this is not a sample-size result.

**Three things were deliberately not done to it.** The gate was **not** restated at a number the
evidence meets; the denominator was **not** narrowed; the corpus was **not** re-split. The gate
file records the FAIL and the owner's decision beside it, and the README's **first screen**
carries 7.1% with its interval, so no one can install this without meeting the number.

**Where the evidence is lost, measured over 56 verification attempts:** 17 probes do not collect
at all; **17 are refused by the process guard on the merge base**, where nothing untrusted runs;
13 fall to the intent clause. **Two mechanical categories hold 34 of 56, and neither is an
adjudicator.** That is the work after this tag, and it is product work rather than corpus work.

## 7. One thing the checklist found on its way to the tag

**The release workflow marked every release `--prerelease`, unconditionally.** It would have
shipped `v0.1.0` as a pre-release however this checklist read — the flag was written when the
only tags were `v0.1.0-pilot.1`, `v0.1.0-rc.1` and `v0.1.0-rc.2`, and nothing revisited it. The
kind is now decided by the tag's own name: a tag carrying a pre-release segment gets
`--prerelease`, a bare `vX.Y.Z` gets `--latest`, and the release notes differ accordingly.

Verified before the tag on three names: `v0.1.0` → `--latest`, `v0.1.0-rc.3` → `--prerelease`,
`v0.2.0-pilot.1` → `--prerelease`.

## 8. What this release is not

- **Not accurate.** 7.1% recall, upper bound 22.6%. It will miss most defects.
- **Not proven useful.** The prospective run published **nothing**; a review that says nothing
  cannot be wrong and cannot be useful, and the gate that passed does not ask for utility.
- **Not measured at the shipped `K`.** Every control number is a **K=4** number. The K=5 red
  control arm (≈$126) and `G-NULL-001`'s full population (≈$53) are **known untested, for budget**
  (D-196).
- **Not externally observed, for 11 of 13 attack classes.** One kernel observer has run once and
  watched seven syscalls.
- **Not on the Marketplace and not on PyPI.** Both are the owner's own steps and neither is taken.
