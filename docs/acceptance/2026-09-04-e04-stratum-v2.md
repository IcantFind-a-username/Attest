# E-04 shadow, stratum v2 — 100 units, 7 shadow findings, and the family rule is why

**Owner instruction 5 of 2026-09-04b.** `benchmarks/studies/e04-prospective-v2`, protocol frozen
at `f2c21332` before any unit ran. 100 units, **100 run, none deferred**, spend
**$11.089240** of the $14 cap. Local review path only: no GitHub client is constructed, so
nothing was published to anyone. Product code: the window tip, including D-124, D-125 and D-126.

> **This stratum is not prospective.** Its units already existed when the protocol was frozen.
> The preregistration, every sample row and the report all carry `prospective: false`, and
> `G-SHADOW-001` — which asks for units the product could not have seen — **is not advanced by
> it**. Stratum v1's two genuinely prospective units remain the only prospective ones. What this
> stratum buys is volume on real recent changes the product had never reviewed.

## 5. Why they published at all: the family rule, measured

Replaying these three units' recorded inputs through the **pre-D-125 PR-wide rule** — the same
offline replay that reproduces 121 of 121 recorded reviews
([data](evidence/2026-09-04-family-replay.json)) — gives:

| unit | eligible `m` | units | PR-wide bar | old rule | **new rule (D-125)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Attest@34affaf` | 7 | 3 | 70 | **0 published** | **1** |
| `us-stock-helper@7245680` | 20 | 8 | 200 | **0 published** | **3** |
| `us-stock-helper@cdf221f` | 16 | 5 | 160 | **0 published** | **3** |

**Under the rule this product shipped yesterday, this entire 100-unit shadow run would have been
silent.** Twenty-one accepted receipts, and not one of them author-visible. That is the clearest
statement yet of what the 2026-09-03 backlog item was about, and it is the reason D-125 exists.

It also transfers the whole risk. A silent product cannot be wrong in public; this one now can.
Whether these seven are right is the question the next window has to answer, and it is a
question about the *findings*, not about the family rule.

## 6. What this stratum does not establish

- **`semantic_precision: INSUFFICIENT`, and it will stay so until someone adjudicates.** All
  seven shadow findings are unadjudicated. The protocol's safety stop is defined on **wrong**
  findings, so it cannot be evaluated: `safety_stop_reached: false` here means "no finding has
  been adjudicated wrong", not "no finding is wrong".
- **The adjudication cannot be done by this agent.** `G-SHADOW-001` requires a product-blind
  audit using evidence independent of the product result. The agent that produced the findings
  is not blind to them and wrote the product; anything it says about them is a reading, not the
  preregistered audit. All seven stay `unresolved`.
- **`eligible_detection: INSUFFICIENT`** — 97 silent units were drawn for the silent audit and
  none is adjudicated, and the preregistered minimum is 100 confirmed eligible opportunities.
- **Not prospective, and not `G-SHADOW-001`'s n.** 100 units across 4 repositories against the
  gate's ≥500 PRs across ≥30 repositories.
- **Two of the four repositories are the product's own and its sibling.** `Attest` supplied 40
  units — a disclosed conflict of interest — and produced one of the seven findings.
- **The spend of the first eleven units understates their true cost.** Those units had already
  run once before the restart, so their proposal samples were served from the attempt cache the
  discarded run populated; the $1.51036 that bought them is charged to the reservation and is not
  in the $11.089240 above.
