## 3. `attest.intent.v6` against the shipped rule, on the same evidence

### 3a. What v6 is (D-252)

A specification may be a **contract** -- an object-valued expected side, a `parametrize` row, or an
exception a base test expects through a *caller* of the touched symbol -- admitted only when it
binds **(i)** the probe's concrete input, argument by argument as literals; **(ii)** a value the
observer derives mechanically (a literal, a `NamedTuple`/`@dataclass` constructor of the tree with
literal fields, or a container of those); **(iii)** the same entry of the anchored module, or,
for a caller contract, the very caller the probe entered through. Every contract found is
recorded with the binding it lacks. Ten tests pin it (`tests/test_intent_v6.py`); the four
counterexamples are the mis-associations it must refuse -- another input, an underivable expected
side, a same-named symbol of another module, a caller the probe did not enter through -- and each
asserts the shipped rule's verdict beside v6's.
